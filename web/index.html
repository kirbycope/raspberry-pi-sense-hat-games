Sense HAT Games

Two games for a Raspberry Pi Sense HAT's 8x8 LEDs, running here in your browser

Pick a game
Sense HAT

The arrow keys move the joystick and Enter or Space presses it in, or use the joystick on the board.
Space Invaders: move left and right, press in to fire. Clear all six invaders.
The Legend of Zelda: walk Link through Tail Cave from Link's Awakening, pick up keys and open doors.
These are the original Python games, run unchanged by Pyodide against a stand-in for the Sense HAT library.
